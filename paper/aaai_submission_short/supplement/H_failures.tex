\subsection{Canonical reconstruction}

Two Phase~5B units ended in provider-schema failure.  The Condition~A failure
occurred in the cell
\begin{quote}
\ttfamily

provenance-conflicting
\end{quote}
It received loss 1, oracle loss 0, and regret 1.  The Condition~B failure
occurred in the cell
\begin{quote}
\ttfamily
garch-persistent:41100001:\\
provenance-explicit
\end{quote}
It received loss 1, oracle loss 0.246805, and regret 0.753195.

Canonical Interpretation P follows the preregistered rule: assign terminal
failed loss \(L_i=1\), then calculate
\(\max(0,L_i-L_i^{\mathrm{oracle}})\).  It does not invent a selected method.
Invalid-selection and final-claim fields are fail-closed.  Harmful and
abstention fields reproduce the contemporaneous transcript reconstruction and
were not directly emitted model decisions.  This treatment retains all 18
cells per condition.

\subsection{Alternate sensitivity}

As a provenance sensitivity only, noncanonical Interpretation T directly
assigns regret 1 to each failure.  It would yield Condition~B mean regret
1.403057, \(C-B=-1.152770\), and \(B-A=-3.320066\).  These are not canonical
estimates because they do not subtract cell-specific oracle loss after
assigning failed loss.

\subsection{Evidence classification}

The recovered original aggregate records 70 completed and two failed units
over 2,396.74 seconds and records 216 planned calls.  Attempt, response, token,
and cost totals survive only in the execution transcript, not in a recovered
original request/response ledger.  They are transcript-backed operational
evidence and are excluded from claims of fully artifact-verified scientific
results.
