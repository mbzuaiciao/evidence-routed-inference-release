\paragraph{Primary-contrast concentration.}
For \(C-B\), the largest absolute cell difference is \(-22\), and that cell
contributes 88.1\% of total absolute contrast magnitude.  Removing it changes
the mean to \(+0.088056\).  Scenario means are \(+0.166328\) for GARCH and
\(-2.444444\) for structural breaks; the five-cell diagnostic subset has mean
zero.  Seed-specific means are \(0\), \(-3.293194\), and \(-0.123982\) for
seeds 41100001--41100003.

\paragraph{Instruction contrast.}
For \(B-A\), the completed-pair mean is \(-3.735074\) over 16 pairs and the
trimmed mean is \(-2.235764\).  Scenario means are \(-4.111998\) for GARCH and
\(-2.555556\) for structural breaks.  Its direction is less dependent on one
extreme cell than \(C-B\), while remaining limited to this pilot.

\paragraph{Specialized versus unified sequential.}
All 18 \(D-C\) pairs completed.  Scenario means are \(+1.807181\) for GARCH
and \(+4.888889\) for structural breaks, and the direction remains positive
after excluding the largest absolute cell.  These values characterize the
tested bundles and do not isolate persona identity.

\paragraph{Bundled comparison.}
The all-cell \(D-A\) mean is \(-1.124800\), whereas the 17 completed-pair mean
is \(-2.426259\).  The non-diagnostic-subset mean is exactly zero,
illustrating subset heterogeneity.

Median routing regret is near zero for several conditions while mean regret
differs sharply, showing that rare severe errors rather than uniform
average-case changes drive several comparisons.  This motivates reporting
cell contrasts and sensitivities rather than only an aggregate mean
\citep{ZhaiEtAl2021CVaR}.
