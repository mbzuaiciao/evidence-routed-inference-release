The adaptive deterministic policy applies fixed diagnostic-acquisition,
routing, and abstention rules and uses no LLM at runtime.  Its advantage over
static routing is concentrated in structural-break cells where acquiring
stability evidence prevents high-loss decisions; GARCH mean regret is
approximately 0.003 for both deterministic policies.  The policy was
co-developed with the benchmark and directly encodes eligibility rules.  It
therefore supplies a formalizability boundary, not evidence that deterministic
systems generally dominate language models or a second primary contribution.

The evidence-constrained Phase~4A row records integration failure and cannot
be read as language-model reasoning quality.  Phase~4J nevertheless retains
all 108 intended outputs, including terminal failures, rather than dropping
them from the denominator.
