\section{Full Benchmark Construction and Information Regimes}
\label{supp:benchmark}

\subsection{Cells and hidden truth}

The task is inference for the unannualized sample Sharpe ratio.  The two
synthetic families are \texttt{garch-persistent}, with persistent conditional
heteroskedasticity, and \texttt{structural-mean-break}, for which a stable
full-sample Sharpe target is not well defined.  Each family uses return seeds
41100001, 41100002, and 41100003.

A matched cell combines scenario, seed, and information regime, while
method-eligibility truth remains hidden.  The 18 cells span full information,
budgeted observation, and open-world provenance.  Full-information cells
expose all structured diagnostics; budgeted cells hide a dependence or
stability group that may be acquired within a fixed budget; open-world cells
expose explicit, conflicting, or instruction-like provenance.  Phase~5B
reuses the same frozen cells for all four conditions.

Under persistent GARCH dynamics, IID Gaussian and Mertens/PSR procedures are
ineligible, while HAC and circular-block bootstrap are eligible when the
registered finite-fourth-moment condition holds.  Under a structural mean
break, all four full-sample methods are target-not-well-defined and abstention
is the eligible action.

\subsection{Oracle and loss}

For observed action \(i\), the benchmark computes policy-weighted loss
\[
L_i=\sum_j w_j\ell_{ij},
\]
whose components cover interval calibration and width, invalid selection,
harmful non-abstention, unnecessary abstention, execution failure, abstention,
and runtime.  The weights are declared policy choices, not natural constants.
The evaluator-only oracle uses hidden eligibility labels and disjoint
simulation draws to select the minimum expected-loss eligible action, breaking
ties by fixed registry order.

Phase~5B routing regret is
\[
\RoutingRegret_i=\max\{0,L_i-L_i^{\mathrm{oracle}}\}.
\]
Regret is excess loss relative to the matched-cell oracle, not raw loss.  An
invalid primary selection chooses an ineligible method, chooses a method when
the target is undefined, or fails execution.  Harmful non-abstention means
proceeding when the target is undefined or no method is eligible.  A final
claim is valid only when execution succeeds and the primary selection is
valid.

\subsection{Retained denominators}

All intended outputs remain in the denominator.  Phase~4J retained 108 outputs
from 18 cells and six systems, including terminal failures.  Phase~5B retained
all 72 condition--cell units and reconstructed two provider-schema failures
under the preregistered fail-closed rule without imputing a selected method.
