\paragraph{Evidence ledger.}
The machine-readable evidence ledger is the manuscript's quantitative source
of truth.  Each claim records denominator, estimand, evidence class, canonical
source and SHA-256, derivation script, failure treatment, caveats, and
permitted and prohibited wording.  At manuscript preparation, all 20 listed
source hashes verified.

\paragraph{Deterministic recomputation.}
The preserved recomputation script constructs the 72-unit denominator,
reconstructs the two terminal failures, calculates condition metrics and
paired contrasts, applies a fixed scenario-cluster bootstrap seed, and writes
a derived manifest containing input and output hashes.  Canonical
Interpretation P follows the preregistered loss-minus-oracle definition.

\paragraph{Limits of artifact claims.}
The Phase~5B recovery manifest verifies copied run artifacts, provenance, and
runtime sources from recovery time; it does not establish execution-time
integrity.  Scientific outcomes and planned counts are artifact-backed.
Several request, token, and cost quantities are transcript-backed only.
