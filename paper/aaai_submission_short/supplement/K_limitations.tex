\section{Additional Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{supp:limitations}

The Phase~5B evaluation contains only 18 cells from two scenario families.
Scenario-cluster uncertainty is estimated from only two clusters and is
unstable \citep{MacKinnonWebb2018}; intervals and contrasts are descriptive,
not conventional population-level significance tests.

Several aggregate differences are driven by rare severe failures.  For
\(C-B\), one structural-break cell accounts for most of the mean advantage,
trimming reverses the sign, and the scenario families favor different
conditions.  The study therefore does not establish a broad
sequential-execution advantage.

The four conditions are not a complete factorial decomposition.  B and C
match substantive instructions and nominal output capacity but differ in call
count, accumulated context, and persisted state.  C and D differ in personas,
message partitioning, schemas, role boundaries, and context flow.  \(D-C\) is
a residual architectural contrast, not a pure persona effect.

The benchmark is narrow and synthetic, focusing on Sharpe-ratio inference
under dependence, heteroskedasticity, distributional, stability, and
provenance conditions.  Results may differ with unstructured evidence,
ambiguous ontologies, heterogeneous tools, distinct expertise, or adversarial
interaction.  The study does not establish that specialized agents are
generally inferior.

All live Phase~5B conditions use one hosted model and provider configuration.
Replication across models, providers, and independently implemented workflows
is required before broader architectural claims.

The adaptive deterministic policy was co-developed with the benchmark.  Its
diagnostics, evidence ontology, eligibility rules, priorities, and abstention
conditions encode domain knowledge aligned with benchmark construction.  Its
performance is a formalizability boundary and engineered reference, not
evidence that deterministic routing universally dominates LLMs.  Although
LLMs assisted policy development, the study does not evaluate autonomous
policy synthesis.

Two units failed provider-schema validation and were reconstructed under the
preregistered fail-closed loss rule.  Some failed-unit abstention and harmful
fields rely on the contemporaneous accounting convention rather than directly
emitted model decisions.  The alternate direct-regret treatment is reported
only as sensitivity.

The recovered evidence package contains primary outcomes, aggregate metrics,
sentinel, authorization, freeze, and transcripts, but not a complete original
request/response and provider-audit ledger.  Operational call, token, and cost
quantities are therefore not all artifact-backed.

Finally, routing regret encodes a prespecified loss profile.  Its magnitude
reflects domain-specific judgments about the costs of invalid decisions and
abstention \citep{Chow1970Reject}.  Alternative weights could alter rankings
or the apparent concentration of differences.
