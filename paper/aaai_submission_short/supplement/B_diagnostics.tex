\section{Diagnostic Definitions and Thresholds}
\label{supp:diagnostics}

\paragraph{Data quality.}
Returns must be nonempty, one-dimensional, and finite without imputation.
Configured sample-size evidence requires at least 30 observations, and
variance at or below \(10^{-12}\) is rejected.  The diagnostic also reports
repeated-value concentration and, when timestamps are supplied, missingness,
duplication, ordering, and spacing irregularity.  Repetition and spacing
cutoffs are heuristic warnings rather than formal market-data tests.

\paragraph{Linear and squared-return dependence.}
Separate diagnostics apply the same Ljung--Box construction to returns and to
squared centered returns.  For \(n\) observations and maximum lag \(h\),
\[
Q=n(n+2)\sum_{k=1}^{h}\frac{\widehat\rho_k^2}{n-k}.
\]
The lags are \(1,2,5,10\), the asymptotic reference level is 0.05, and
dependence is also flagged when any absolute autocorrelation reaches 0.15.
The minimum sample size is 30.  Squared-return dependence is evidence of
volatility clustering, not identification of a particular volatility model;
failure to detect dependence does not prove independence.

\paragraph{Distribution shape.}
The implementation uses uncorrected plug-in skewness
\(\widehat\gamma_3\), raw Pearson kurtosis \(\widehat\gamma_4\), and
\[
JB=\frac{n}{6}\left\{\widehat\gamma_3^2+
\frac{(\widehat\gamma_4-3)^2}{4}\right\}.
\]
Material non-Gaussianity is flagged at absolute skewness 0.75, raw kurtosis
6.0, or asymptotic \(p<0.05\), with minimum \(n=30\).  A separate
fourth-moment item is always inconclusive because a finite sample cannot
establish a finite population fourth moment.

\paragraph{Split-sample stability.}
At a 50\% split, the diagnostic compares segment means using their estimated
standard error and compares sample variances by the larger-to-smaller ratio.
It flags instability when the absolute standardized mean difference is at
least 3 or the variance ratio is at least 3.  Evaluation requires \(n\geq60\)
and at least 20 observations per segment.  These are policy thresholds, not a
calibrated change-point test.

\paragraph{Research provenance.}
Provenance records trial and parameter-search counts, searched markets and
windows, failed-trial retention, trial-dependence status, completeness, and a
reference.  ``Complete'' is accepted only when all required fields are
present.  Incomplete provenance blocks deflated-Sharpe claims; complete
provenance does not enable them because the audited adjustment method is not
implemented.

\paragraph{Visibility and acquisition.}
Evidence is grouped as returns summary, linear dependence, squared dependence,
distribution, stability, and structured provenance.  A cell exposes only its
declared groups.  Budgeted systems may reveal registered groups up to the cell
budget; the adaptive deterministic policy uses the priority squared
dependence, stability, linear dependence, then distribution.  Ground-truth
eligibility labels are generated separately and never exposed as diagnostic
evidence.
